# Album sleeves

Twenty four covers, collaged behind the contact room. Each `<figure>` in
`index.html` names its file, and a missing file removes its whole figure, so the
pile closes over any gap.

The pile is whole rather than holed: scales disagree by more than double, pieces
overlap edge to edge so no background shows through, and a scrim above them
darkens the middle so the words sit on the collage instead of in a gap cut out
of it. Treatments cycle so no two neighbours match: torn from paper with the
white deckle left on (`lp--paper`), ripped out with no border (`lp--tear`),
printed in one ink (`lp--duo`), screened into halftone dots (`lp--halftone`),
or left as it came. Newsprint grain sits over everything.

Square, 400x400 or larger. Cover art belongs to the labels, so it stays
decorative here: no titles printed, no artist credits, nothing sold.
